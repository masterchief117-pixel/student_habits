#   STUDENT HABIT TRACKER
A simple project that helps students record their daily activities which includes:
        - Timetables
        -Assignments
        - Habits 
It also comes with bible verse to start your day through communication from the Bible and remember what god says

## key features
**time-table** :- create your weekly timetable. You can always update your time table,add new 
                  activities or even unwanted one
                - Key in activities into the timetable is simple because you can multi input, just add your activity and choose the days you want the activity to appear
**Assigments**:- add assignments  with their units and unit codes and the task you are to handle
               - add due dates  to this assignment and you have a reminder that shows you how many assignments you are yet to handle                    
**Habit** : - here you create your habit and its occurence based on a daily basis or a weekly basis
            - you also have a check box for your habit,,you can mark it as done, partial done or grace day for a busy day and lastly you can drop the habit if it is no longer needed
